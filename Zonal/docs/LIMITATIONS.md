# Limitations

- One saved 2019 weather chronology provides deterministic scenario operation, not multi-year probabilistic adequacy. Zero physical shedding does not establish an adequacy standard.
- Seven-zone aggregation omits within-zone transmission constraints. Modeled directional-interface saturation is not observed AC-line overload.
- Capacities and annual demand are fixed scenario assumptions. No investment equilibrium, endogenous expansion or network expansion is solved.
- Synthetic external markets and accepted boundary prices approximate a wider system. The v6 external EUR 400 treatment is not an Italian price cap.
- UC physical dispatch and fixed-commitment LP prices answer different questions. Prices are conditional on accepted commitment; pricing-LP primal operation is not canonical physical generation.
- Historical version comparisons include multiple changes and cannot automatically be interpreted as single-factor causal experiments.
- Public tables support the advertised numerical analysis and selected figure programme. Full solved networks/raw KKT arrays, abandoned offer-stack visuals, experimental PPT alternatives and raw source research reconstruction are not distributed or claimed reproducible by this route.
- A future Nodal model could address more detailed physical network representation; it has not been implemented by this publication.
